Milestones · 2008 → today

Seventeen years, one direction: forward.

From a single outpatient clinic to a multi-wing hospital. The milestones we're proudest of are the ones our patients felt first.

Current capital projects
Founded
2008

First clinic opens in Bole

Addis Medical Center launches as a 12-room outpatient clinic with 4 consultants and one shared laboratory. Same-day appointments from day one.

Expansion
2012

Garden Wing opens — pediatrics & maternity

A new 48-bed wing dedicated to children and mothers. The hospital delivers its 1,000th baby the following spring.

Technology
2015

First 1.5T MRI and CT scanner on-site

Imaging moves in-house — ending the cross-town referral loop for brain, spine and abdominal scans. Radiologist on-site 7 days a week.

Accreditation
2018

ISO 15189 laboratory accreditation

Our pathology laboratory becomes one of the first in Ethiopia to earn ISO 15189 — independent verification of every result we report.

Emergency
2020

24/7 emergency & ambulance service

Adult, pediatric and cardiac emergency bays open around the clock, with public ambulance line 907. Over 3,200 emergency visits in the first year.

Quality
2022

JCI-aligned quality program

Independent audit against Joint Commission International standards across patient safety, infection control and medication management.

Growth
2023

3.0T wide-bore MRI installed

A 70 cm open-bore scanner — the first of its kind in a private Addis hospital — removing the enclosed feeling of older MRI systems.

Digital
2024

Patient portal goes live

Results, scans, bills and prescriptions in one place. 68% of returning patients now use the portal to manage their care.

Today
2025

180 beds · 42 consultants · 8 specialties

The largest private hospital footprint we've ever operated — and still booking most specialist visits within the same week.
