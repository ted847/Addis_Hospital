Who we are

Healthcare built around people, not just appointments.

Addis Medical Center is a patient-focused healthcare provider serving the Addis Ababa community and beyond. Our purpose is to make quality healthcare easier to access through coordinated medical services, knowledgeable Ethiopian healthcare professionals and a respectful patient experience.

We bring different areas of care together — from consultations and specialist assessment to diagnostic support, treatment, emergency attention and follow-up. We aim to help patients and families understand their care, make informed decisions and feel supported throughout their healthcare journey.

Patient-centred careRespect, clear communication and attention to each patient's needs.

Your visit

What happens when you arrive.

Four simple steps from front desk to your doctor — no confusion, no queue surprises.

Full visitor guide

Reception

Check in at the front desk. New patients will be asked for medical history and ID.

Triage

Vital signs checked, a quick consultation, and we route you to the right specialty.

Cashier

Transparent payment handling. Insurance or self-pay — you'll get an itemized estimate.

OPD Station

Payment confirmed, and you're added to your doctor's waiting list. We'll call your name.
