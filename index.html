Who we are

Healthcare built around people, not just appointments.

Addis Medical Center is a patient-focused healthcare concept for Addis Ababa and beyond — bringing consultations, specialist assessment, diagnostic imaging, laboratory science, treatment and follow-up into one respectful experience.

We believe patients should understand their care, make informed decisions, and never feel lost in the system.

Your visit

What happens when you arrive.

Four simple steps from front desk to your doctor — no confusion, no queue surprises.

Reception

Check in at the front desk. New patients bring ID and any previous medical history.

Triage

Vital signs checked, a quick consultation, and we route you to the right specialty.

Diagnostics

Lab, ECG, X-ray, ultrasound or MRI on site — results flow straight to your doctor.

See your doctor

Unhurried consultation, a clear plan, and follow-up arranged before you leave.
