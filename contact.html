Quick answers

Before you come in.

What should I bring?

ID, previous test results, a list of current medications, and insurance details if you have them.

Do I need insurance?

No. Self-pay is welcome with itemised estimates before every procedure. Insurers listed are illustrative — confirm coverage with the centre.

Which languages?

Amharic, English, Afaan Oromo, Tigrinya, Arabic and French — interpreters can be arranged on request.
