Our journey

Milestones that shaped us.

From a 40-bed clinic in 2008 to a regional referral centre.

2008

Horizon opens its doors

Founded as a 40-bed general hospital with 6 consultants and a single operating theatre.

2012

First accreditation

Received national quality certification and opened the first dedicated maternity wing.

2016

Expansion to 120 beds

Added cardiology, orthopedics, and a 24/7 emergency department with trauma bays.

2020

Digital transformation

Launched the Horizon Patient Portal, telemedicine, and e-prescriptions across all departments.

2023

JCI Gold Accreditation

Awarded the Joint Commission International gold seal for patient safety and clinical quality.

2025

East Wing groundbreaking

Began construction on a 120-bed expansion with six new operating theatres and a research centre.
