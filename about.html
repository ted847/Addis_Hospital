Company history

Seventeen years, one direction.

From a single outpatient clinic to a multi-wing hospital on Bole Road.

2008 — The beginning

Horizon opens as a 12-room outpatient clinic with four consultants and one shared laboratory. Same-day appointments from day one.

2012 — Garden Wing

A new 48-bed wing dedicated to children and mothers. The hospital delivers its 1,000th baby the following spring.

2018 — ISO 15189 accreditation

Our pathology laboratory becomes one of the first in Ethiopia to earn ISO 15189 — independent verification of every result.

2023 — 3.0T wide-bore MRI

A 70 cm open-bore scanner — the first of its kind in a private Addis hospital — removing the enclosed feeling of older MRI systems.

2025 — Today

180 beds, 42 senior consultants, 8 clinical departments. Still booking most specialist visits within the same week.

Vision & mission

What drives us.

Our vision

To be the most trusted private hospital in Ethiopia — where clinical excellence and human dignity are inseparable.

Our mission

To deliver calm, clear, coordinated care — with the technology, people and systems to make every visit feel safe and unhurried.

Our values

Patient first, always. Clarity over complexity. Dignity in every interaction. Evidence over marketing. Team over hierarchy.
