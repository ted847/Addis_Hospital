Our approach

Healthcare that explains, listens and follows through.

Addis Medical Center is a patient-focused healthcare concept for Addis Ababa and beyond. Our purpose is to make quality healthcare easier to access through coordinated services, knowledgeable professionals and a respectful patient experience.

We bring different areas of care together — from consultations and specialist assessment to diagnostic support, treatment, emergency attention and follow-up — so patients and families can understand their care and make informed decisions.

  • Respect, clear communication and attention to each patient's needs
  • Specialist teams and services working together for continuity of care
  • Practical information and digital tools for patients
  • Guidance and follow-up that continue after the visit

Company history

From a first consultation room to a modern centre.

Illustrative milestones from the original concept — verify dates and facts before launch.

2008

The first doors open

Addis Medical Center begins with a small OPD team and one promise: no patient should ever feel rushed or unheard.

2014

Specialist departments grow

Cardiology, pediatrics, orthopedics and maternity become dedicated departments with their own teams and equipment.

2019

The imaging centre opens

MRI, CT and full ultrasound suites come online, bringing advanced diagnostics to Addis Ababa without long referral waits.

2022

Emergency & surgical expansion

New operating theatres, recovery suites and a round-the-clock emergency pathway are added to the centre.

2025

Digital patient experience

Online booking, digital reports and a multilingual patient experience become part of everyday care.

Vision & mission

What we aim for, and what we do every day.

Our Vision

Healthcare that feels human.

A future where every person in Addis Ababa receives world-class care that is respectful, understandable and close to home — where technology serves the patient, never the other way around.

Our Mission

Care, made accessible.

To deliver coordinated, high-quality healthcare through knowledgeable teams, modern diagnostics and transparent communication — so every patient understands their care and feels supported at every step.

RespectEvery patient is treated with dignity, in their language, at a human pace.
ClarityDiagnoses, costs and next steps explained before decisions are made.
ContinuityTeams and departments working together across the whole journey.
AccountabilityMeasured outcomes, published standards and honest communication.
