Our journey

Fifteen years of getting better.

2008

Founded on Bole Road

Opened as a 40-bed community hospital on Bole Road.

Campus Milestone 40-Bed Community Foundation
  1. 2008

    Opened as a 40-bed community hospital on Bole Road.

  2. 2014

    Added the maternity wing and a 24-hour emergency department.

  3. 2019

    Opened the imaging centre with CT and MRI — same-day reporting for most studies.

  4. 2021

    Introduced video consultations and the patient portal for records, bills and prescriptions.

  5. 2024

    Launched the multilingual care program: Amharic, English, Oromo, Tigrinya, Arabic, French.

What we stand for

Three things we won't compromise on.

Time that respects you

Average consultation is 24 minutes. Enough to be heard, not just seen. Booking takes under a minute — three taps from first visit to confirmed appointment.

Prices you can see

Itemized estimate before every procedure. Published outcome data every year. If the bill doesn't match the estimate, we eat the difference.

Language that includes

Six languages spoken on call — Amharic, English, Oromo, Tigrinya, Arabic, French. Interpreters available for every consultation, at no extra cost.

Governance

How we stay accountable.

Accreditation

National Standard

Accredited by the national health authority. Annual quality and patient-safety audits are published each year and available on request. Our patient-safety committee meets monthly and reports to the board.

Monthly Safety Committee · Board Oversight

Outcomes & reporting

Published Annually

We publish infection rates, readmission rates and patient-experience scores annually. You can request the latest report at quality@horizonhospital.example.

Leadership

Clinician-Led

Our clinical leadership is drawn from senior consultants in each department. The board includes independent members and a patient representative who rotates annually.

Rotating Patient Board Seat · Independent Governance

Hiring Year-Round
