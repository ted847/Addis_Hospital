How to apply

Clear steps. Respectful timelines.

We review every application directly with department leads — no automated black holes.

1

Send your CV & cover letter

Submit online through our quick-application studio or send your CV and cover letter to careers@horizonhospital.example.

2

Include the role title

Include the exact role title in the subject line so your credentials reach the right department chair immediately.

3

10-working-day response

We respond to shortlisted candidates within 10 working days to schedule a clinical conversation and Bole Road campus tour.

4

Registration & references

All clinical roles require current professional registration and two verified clinical references prior to onboarding.

Credentialing checklist

Have these items ready when applying for clinical or allied health roles at Horizon:

  • Updated Curriculum Vitae (CV) & brief clinical cover letter
  • Active Ethiopian Health Professionals / EFDA / MOH license
  • Specialty board or degree certifications
  • Contact details for two professional clinical referees
