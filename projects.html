The Advanced Imaging Centre

A dedicated suite bringing 1.5T MRI, multi-slice CT and full ultrasound under one roof — so patients in Addis Ababa no longer wait months for scans. Every scan is interpreted by our own radiology team and reported digitally.

Surgical & Emergency Expansion

Two new operating theatres, a recovery suite and a round-the-clock emergency pathway. The expansion cut waiting times for urgent procedures and gave surgeons a fully modern working environment.

The Community Health Program

Free quarterly screening days in the surrounding neighbourhoods — blood pressure, diabetes, eye and child growth checks — followed by a clear referral path into our OPD when it is needed.
