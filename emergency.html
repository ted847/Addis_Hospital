Warning Signs

When to come to the ER

Seek immediate emergency evaluation if you or someone with you experiences any of the following symptoms:

  • Chest pain or pressure
  • Difficulty breathing
  • Severe bleeding that won't stop
  • Sudden weakness or slurred speech
  • Head injury with confusion or vomiting
  • Severe abdominal pain
  • High fever in an infant under 3 months
