Step by step

What happens when you arrive.

Four stations. Clear handoffs. No confusion.

Reception

Check in at the front desk. New patients will be asked for medical history and ID.

Triage

Vital signs checked, a quick consultation, and we route you to the right specialty.

Cashier

Transparent payment handling. Insurance or self-pay — you'll get an itemized estimate.

OPD Station

Payment confirmed, and you're added to your doctor's waiting list. We'll call your name.

Visiting hours

  • General wards: 11:00–13:00 and 16:00–19:00, daily
  • Maternity: 16:00–19:00, daily
  • Intensive care: 17:00–18:00, immediate family only
  • Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult

What to bring

  • Photo ID and insurance card (if applicable)
  • Referral letter and previous test results
  • List of current medications
  • Comfortable clothing for day procedures

Parking & transport

  • Visitor parking on Level B1 and B2, first 2 hours free
  • Accessible bays near the main entrance
  • Taxi drop-off at the main entrance canopy
  • Shuttle from Bole junction every 30 minutes

Arriving for surgery

  • Report to the admission desk 2 hours before your scheduled time
  • Nothing to eat 6 hours before general anesthesia
  • Bring your admission letter and ID
  • Arrange a companion for discharge
