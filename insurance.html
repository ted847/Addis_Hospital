How Billing Works

Three calm steps from check-in to checkout.

Verify at reception

Present your insurance card and photo ID 15 minutes before your appointment so we can confirm active policy limits.

Approve your written estimate

If labs, imaging, or a procedure are recommended, you see exact insurer coverage and any co-pay before proceeding.

Direct claim settlement

We submit signed claim forms straight to your insurer and send an itemized digital receipt to your patient portal.

What to bring to your visit

Having these items ready keeps registration under five minutes.

  • Valid insurance card and active policy / member number
  • Government ID, passport, or driver's license
  • Pre-authorization / guarantee letter for planned surgeries or MRI/CT scans
  • Primary physician referral letter if required by your plan tier

Common Questions

Insurance & billing answers.

What happens in an emergency if I don't have my insurance card with me?

Emergency triage and stabilization always begin immediately without waiting for paperwork. Once you are stable, a family member or our 24/7 triage coordinator can verify your policy number by phone with your insurer.

How long does pre-authorization take for planned procedures or advanced imaging?

Most partner insurers respond to outpatient imaging requests within 2–4 hours, and planned inpatient admissions within 24 hours. Submitting your specialist's request at least two days ahead ensures smooth scheduling.

Can I claim reimbursement if my insurer is international or not listed above?

Yes. We provide stamped clinical summaries, diagnostic reports, and itemized English receipts formatted for international, diplomatic, and NGO medical reimbursement claims.

Unsure what your plan covers?

Share your insurer name and planned service with our billing team before your visit for a clear breakdown.
